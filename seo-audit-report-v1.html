Critical Findings Fix now

C-1 · Hardcoded, unverifiable testimonials + "10K+ / 4.9★ / 99% success" claims

All 22 reviews are static JSX (no dates, profile links, or platform attribution); headline stats have no methodology. On a YMYL site handling ₹85k–₹5L transactions, unverifiable social proof is the strongest negative trust signal under the Sept 2025 QRG.

Falsify: open the GBP listing (CID 0x94ba7b774d9ffdb9) — if Google shows <50 reviews, the "10K+" claim is a credibility gap. Monitor: weekly Google review count (target ≥3/week).

C-2 · IATA / TAAI / Hajj-operator credentials are text-only

No badge images, membership numbers, or link to the IATA public agency directory — for the exact credentials that justify the purchase.

Falsify: no official-issuer <img> or verification <a> on /about. Monitor: "Accreditation" attribute appearing in the brand Knowledge Panel.

C-3 · No legal pages (Privacy / Terms / Cancellation-Refund)

The site collects names, phones, emails and financial inquiries via /api/inquiries with no policy pages — a hard QRG failure and a DPDPA compliance gap in India.

Fix: create the three pages, link in footer. Binary check.

High Findings ~1 week / this sprint

H-1 · Commercial content is client-rendered / thin

Tour & visa detail pages fetch body content in useEffect (skeleton on first paint); listing pages are filter grids whose only crawlable prose is a collapsed FAQ (<100 words). Filter state (?country=Dubai) isn't a crawlable URL, and there are no per-country / per-destination hub pages.

Falsify: curl -A Googlebot /visas — if pricing isn't in the HTML, content is JS-gated. Monitor: GSC "Crawled/Discovered – currently not indexed" for /tours/*, /visas/*.

H-2 · No aggregateRating schema anywhere

No star ratings in local pack or organic snippets (a 15–25% CTR handicap at equal rank). Depends on C-1 (need real review data first).

H-3 · Schema type misuse: Product on tours & visas

Should be TouristTrip (tours) and Service/GovernmentService (visa/attestation). Product forfeits travel-rich-result eligibility.

H-4 · Performance — LCP & TBT likely failing on mobile

HeroSlideCarousel is dynamic(…, {ssr:false}) (hero absent from SSR → est. LCP 3.5–5.5s); the cobe/three.js Globe (28k mapSamples, main-thread WebGL) drives est. TBT 400–900ms / INP 200–400ms. R3F/drei (~200KB) may bleed into public chunks.

Highest-ROI single change: remove ssr:false on HeroSlideCarousel (Swiper 11 is SSR-safe). Monitor: CrUX p75 LCP/INP; Lighthouse LCP-element + TBT in CI.

H-5 · Anonymous blog authorship + near-empty blog

authorId is nullable, falls back to site name; the live blog has one post with body "Hello" — yet sits at sitemap priority 0.9. YMYL blog content needs a credentialed human author.

H-6 · /attestations page-type mismatch (lowest SXO score, 33/100)

A compliance/process query served as a card grid. The correct HRD→MEA→Embassy→MOFA process + per-document timelines exist only inside a collapsed FAQ.

H-7 · No Tier-1 India citations

No JustDial, Sulekha, IndiaMART, or TripAdvisor presence; the GBP URL isn't in sameAs. Table stakes for Hyderabad local search.

H-8 · llms.txt absent

Zero-code static file; the cheapest AI-visibility win (Perplexity especially).
