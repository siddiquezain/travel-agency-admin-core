Net assessment

The team has worked through the v1 plan effectively: 3 of 3 “Wave 1/2” structural items verified done (legal pages, SSR detail content, schema-type migration) plus llms.txt and breadcrumbs — driving the biggest gains in AI-readiness, on-page and schema. The remaining ceiling is unchanged: trust/E-E-A-T (testimonials, credentials, reviews) and thin listing-page content — which is why Content Quality barely moved (+1). The one regression — the 404 OG/logo image — is the cheapest possible fix and should be the very next action.
